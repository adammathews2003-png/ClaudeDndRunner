# Ability Checks & DCs (table reference)

## DC ladder
| Difficulty | DC |
|---|---|
| Very easy | 5 |
| Easy | 10 |
| Medium | 15 |
| Hard | 20 |
| Very hard | 25 |
| Nearly impossible | 30 |

## When to call for a check
Only when the outcome is uncertain AND failure is meaningful. Otherwise auto-succeed
(or auto-fail with narration). Don't allow endless retries: a failed check means that
approach is spent unless circumstances change ("you can't pick this lock — with these
tools, in this light").

## Rolling
d20 + ability modifier + proficiency bonus (if proficient in the skill/tool).
- **Advantage/Disadvantage:** roll 2d20, take higher/lower. They don't stack; one source
  of each cancels to a straight roll.
- **Contests:** both sides roll opposed checks. **House rule: ties go to the PC**
  (RAW says defender; see `house-rules.md`).
- **Group checks:** everyone rolls; succeed if at least half succeed.
- **Working together (Help):** one PC helps another who could plausibly do it alone →
  the doer rolls with advantage.

## Common pairings
Sneak: Dexterity (Stealth) vs. passive Perception · Lie: Charisma (Deception) vs.
Wisdom (Insight) · Search: Wisdom (Perception) for noticing, Intelligence
(Investigation) for deducing · Recall lore: Intelligence (History/Arcana/Religion/Nature)
