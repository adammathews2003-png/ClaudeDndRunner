# Perception, Passive Scores & Hidden Things

## Passive Perception
10 + Wisdom (Perception) modifier (+5 with advantage on such checks, −5 with
disadvantage). Stored in each PC's frontmatter (`passive-perception:`).

**GM protocol on entering a scene:** compare every `## Hidden` entry's DC in the location
file against each PC's passive Perception. Anything at-or-below a PC's passive score is
woven into the narration for that PC automatically — no roll, no request. This is how
players "just notice" things.

## Active Perception rolls
Ask for a roll when a PC actively searches, or when something is about to happen that
alert characters might catch (ambush, pickpocket, eavesdropper). Ask BEFORE revealing
why: "Kira, roll Perception." If the request itself would spoil (e.g., detecting an
ongoing pickpocket), the GM rolls secretly and narrates only the outcome.

## Hiding & ambush
A hiding creature rolls Dexterity (Stealth); it's noticed by anyone whose passive
Perception meets the roll, or who beats it with an active check while searching.
Surprised creatures (unaware at initiative) can't act on their first combat turn.

## Light & senses (quick table)
- Dim light: disadvantage on Perception checks relying on sight (−5 effective passive).
- Darkness: effectively blinded without darkvision.
- Darkvision: see in darkness as dim light (so still the −5), in shades of gray.
