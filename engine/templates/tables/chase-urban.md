# Chase complications — urban

| roll  | result                                                                                          | effect |
|-------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------|
| 1     | A handcart tips over in the lane: DC 10 DEX (Acrobatics) to vault it, or lose 10 ft              | -10 ft on a fail |
| 2     | A crowd spills out of a temple door: DC 10 STR (Athletics) to shove through, or lose 10 ft       | -10 ft on a fail |
| 3     | Washing lines strung low between houses: DC 10 DEX save or tangled, lose 10 ft                    | -10 ft on a fail |
| 4     | A gap between rooftops or a fence in the way: DC 10 STR (Athletics) to clear it, or lose 15 ft   | -15 ft on a fail |
| 5     | Slick cobbles from a spilled barrel: DC 10 DEX save or fall prone                                 | prone on a fail |
| 6     | A street seller grabs a sleeve, insisting on a sale: DC 10 CHA (Persuasion) or lose 10 ft         | -10 ft on a fail |
| 7     | A side alley opens up: the quarry can duck into it                                                | los |
| 8     | A dog bolts out barking and snapping: DC 10 DEX save or 1d4 piercing                              | 1d4 piercing on a fail |
| 9     | A watch patrol turns the corner and shouts a challenge                                            | narrate |
| 10    | Steam and smoke from a forge door fill the street: the quarry vanishes in it                      | los |
| 11-20 | No complication                                                                                 |        |
