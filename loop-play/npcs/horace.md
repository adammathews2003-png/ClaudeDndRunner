# Horace

## Description
A very large grey goose with a dented beak and the unhurried stare of something that
has seen everything. Follows Wil at a distance of about forty feet, like a bodyguard
who has been told not to make it obvious. Hisses at nothing, honks at exactly the wrong
moment, and is always, somehow, on a roof or a wall.

## Attacks
| name | hit | damage          | range | notes                                              |
|------|-----|-----------------|-------|----------------------------------------------------|
| bite | +3  | 1d4 bludgeoning | 5     | plus a grip: DEX save DC 10 or drop a held small item |

Fly 40 ft. Honk (action): every sleeping creature within 120 ft wakes.

## Personality & motivation
Patient, vindictive, methodical. He likes things as they are. He has a routine and the
routine is perfect: breakfast, menace a child, nap on the bell tower, steal something
shiny, be fed by the king at the feast. He does not want anything about it to change.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: nothing; he is a goose
- **He remembers.** Horace was in Wil's arms when the wish was made and loops with him. He understands Common well enough and has had 4,200 days to learn where everything is. He knows where all four stones are, what the Crown is, and that the party is a threat. He acts on it: pilfers small bright things from packs (Sleight of Hand +4; DC 13 Perception to notice), leads anyone following him the long way round, honks inside the Hollow Glacier if the party nears the deep hall (wakes Olaf), and at the ceremony goes for whichever stone is nearest to being pressed into the Crown
- If killed: he is back at 06:00, remembering it, and considerably less subtle about the party. He cannot be reasoned with; he can be bribed, once per loop, with a whole cheese (he keeps his side of a bargain for about an hour)
- The party can learn he remembers by DC 14 Insight (Animal Handling also works) on his behaviour, or by catching him twice with the same thing
