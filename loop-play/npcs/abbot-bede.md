# Abbot Bede

## Description
Vast, serene, bald as a cask, with a voice like warm gravel and a tankard he never
seems to empty. Moves at the speed of a tide. Listens to everything you say and then
says "mm" in a way that makes you feel it was worth saying.

## Personality & motivation
Patient to a fault, which is the Order's whole idea. Nothing is urgent; everything will
be got to. He is quietly pleased with the Great Toast's length, which he regards as a
spiritual achievement, and he is fond of Brother Grunt, whom the monks found snowed
under on the Serpent's Stair and have fed ever since. He would never let a guest go
hungry or a toast go unfinished. He will trade the stone for a good speech.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: the ale, the rules of the Great Toast (18:00; three rounds; the winner takes a cask and the Abbot's favour), the Order's history ("we were founded by a man who waited for bread to rise and never stopped"), Grunt's story, and that Grunt "ate something in the mash on Day 1, a stone, Brother Pell says; it has done him no harm; he fizzes"
- Needs persuasion (DC 14) or the Toast won: he will have Grunt bring the stone up "when he is ready, which with mustard-water is about ten minutes"
- Needs trust: he has noticed the toast come round to the same words, and he finds it beautiful rather than alarming; told it is a loop, he says "mm", and then "then I have been patient for a very long time; I am glad," and helps with anything, slowly
