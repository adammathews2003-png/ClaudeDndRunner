# Crown Princess Ysolde

## Description
Twenty-two, sharp-faced, in riding clothes under the ceremonial sash because nobody
told her not to. Reads during speeches. Has a laugh like a bark that she tries to
swallow. Carries a small knife, a smaller book, and a very large opinion of her father's
festival.

## Personality & motivation
Clever, bored, decent. She finds Midwinter embarrassing and the goose-herder sweet.
Would like, more than anything, something to happen. If the party gives her a problem
worth solving she will throw herself at it with a competence that alarms the Guard. She
would never betray her father; she will lie to him for a good cause without blinking.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: the Keep's layout, including the service door behind the dais tapestry and the musicians' stair; that the chapel guards are "Bergmann's, so they will actually stand there"; that the Crown "is heavier than it looks and warm, which is horrid"
- Needs persuasion (DC 12) or a shared drink: she smiled at the goose-herder on Day 1 "because he was so happy, it was like watching a dog see the sea" and has felt oddly guilty about it ever since this morning
- Needs trust (ally): she has noticed the day is wrong. Not the loop; she does not remember. But the frost on the bell is the same shape as a dream she keeps having, and the Queen effigy's eyes follow her. She will help with anything that promises an explanation, including a distraction at 20:45
