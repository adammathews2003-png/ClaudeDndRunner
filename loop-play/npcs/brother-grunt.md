# Brother Grunt

## Description
An ogre in a monk's habit made of three monk's habits, with a rope belt and a wooden
spoon on it like a holy symbol. Enormously gentle, enormously slow, and currently a
little luminous: he burps small amber sparks and hiccups applause. Speaks about forty
words of Common and all of them politely.

## Personality & motivation
He was lost on the Serpent's Stair in the first snow and the monks dug him out, fed
him, and gave him a name and a bed. He is grateful in the way of a very large dog. He
wants the next meal and for nobody to shout. He would never hurt a monk. He will hit
someone who hits him, once, to see if they stop.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: "Grunt ate rock. Rock fizzy. Grunt sorry." He knows the stone is in him, that it tingles when people make speeches, and that the Abbot says it will come out "when ready"
- Needs a sausage: he will try to bring it up on request; this requires mustard-water (the kitchen has it) and a Medicine check DC 12, or a very long wait; he is embarrassed about it
- Never: nothing secret; he is a plain soul who swallowed a wish's leftovers
