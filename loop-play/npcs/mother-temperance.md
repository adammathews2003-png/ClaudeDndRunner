# Mother Temperance Quill

## Description
Sixty, small, straight-backed, in grey wool with a ladle at her belt like a mace. Eyes
like two pokers. Does not raise her voice because she has never needed to. Disapproves
of Midwinter on principle and attends it every year to disapprove in person.

## Personality & motivation
Stern, fair, quietly funny, impossible to shock. She keeps the fire because someone
must and the soup because people are hungry. She would never refuse sanctuary. She
will refuse nonsense, loudly, and the Crown of Misrule is, in her view, nonsense of
the oldest kind.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: soup; sanctuary; the Hymnal's Midwinter song, read aloud with disdain, including the verse about the five stones; that the Crown "is older than the kingdom and should be in a hole"; that the First Winter King's shrine in the crypt is "a goose, don't ask me, I only keep the place"
- Needs persuasion (DC 13): the Crown is a fey thing. "It grants a day as king and one wish for the day, and the rhyme says the king is sent on his way at midnight. In the old days I expect that meant a ducking. In older days I expect it did not." She knows the Toast, Dance, Song and Morning-After as the four faces of a feast: "and Prudence to say when to stop"
- Needs trust (ally), or evidence of the loop: she will say what "deposed" means in the oldest rite she knows: "the king gives back the day, and the day takes back what it gave him." She reads that as memory. She is right, and she will say so plainly, and then ask what the party means to do about it
