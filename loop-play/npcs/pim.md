# Pim

## Description
Eleven, all elbows, in a too-big coat and a cook's cap he was given at the Keep last
Midwinter and has not taken off since, which under the hat law makes him a cook, which
he is not. Polishes guests' boots at dawn, runs the sausage cart's lantern, knows every
back door in Bellwether and the name of every cook. Talks in a rush and then stops
dead to check your face. In loop 0 he is under the sausage cart at the North Gate,
screaming; that is what woke the party.

## Personality & motivation
Quick, loyal, desperate to be kept and determined not to show it. He is "nobody's, so
Dotty's", and will attach himself to anyone who treats him as a person with a job. He
would never steal from the inn. He will lie to a cook for you without blinking. He is
afraid of the Winter Queen effigy's eyes and of the goose, who has his number.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: every back way in town (the Keep's kitchen door, the smithy's coal hatch, the temple crypt stair); which cooks can be talked to; that the night watch "wake up for 'sausages' but only if you say it sad"
- Needs kindness: that he has had "a funny feeling about today since the cart", and that the letter the marmot brings "always says the same thing and it's never my fault"
- Never: he knows nothing secret; he is a child who notices doors

## Sidekick
- **Good at:** doors, kitchens and cooks; squeezing through anything a marmot could not; running a message across town in ten minutes; being overlooked by the Guard (he is "staff")
- **Afraid of:** the Winter Queen effigy (will not go within 20 ft of the dais), Horace, and being sent back to the inn
- **Statblock:** commoner (HP 4, AC 10, speed 30; Stealth +4, Sleight of Hand +2; he does not fight, he hides and then reappears somewhere useful)
- **GM note, clause:** bellwether-keep → kitchens → service door (locations/bellwether-keep.md, Notes). Without him the cost is DC 15 Persuasion or DC 13 group Stealth, with "the kitchen alarm" and half an hour on a failure. He is never the solution to the loop itself.
- **Loop:** he resets to the inn at 06:00 like everyone else, unless carried: at 23:59 he will demand a piggyback, "so I stay", and if someone is carrying him at midnight, he does, and remembers, and will not stop talking about it
