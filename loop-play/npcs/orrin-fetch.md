# Orrin Fetch

## Description
A long, loose-jointed man in a coat of forty pockets, with a pedlar's cart snowed into
the Marmot's stable yard and a sign on its tailgate: "FETCH'S FINDS: EVERYTHING MUST GO
(IT WON'T FIT UP THE PASS)". Talks in questions. Has a magnifying glass on a string
that he uses to look at coins, never at goods.

## Personality & motivation
Cheerful, evasive, genuinely knowledgeable about things he claims to know nothing
about. He came over the Southgate Pass with the party's caravan and is the only other
person at the inn who is not from Caldermont. He wants to sell everything and leave in
spring. He would never sell a thing he knew was cursed without mentioning it, quietly,
afterwards. He does not haggle; he "reconsiders".

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: what everything on the cart "probably" does; gossip from the pass (Hob's Rest "had smoke coming out of it when we passed, and I didn't stop to ask"); that the wand of magic detection on his cart "points at the Keep, which is a fault"
- Needs persuasion (DC 12): his coin-box is "full again every morning, which is not a complaint"; he has noticed his stock changes overnight and assumed it was the goose
- Never: nothing secret; he is a pedlar who is being looped like everyone else, and is making money at it
