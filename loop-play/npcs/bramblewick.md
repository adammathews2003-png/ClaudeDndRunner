# Bramblewick

## Description
A barrel-chested satyr with frost in his beard, a crown of birch twigs, and a ruby
wired to his left horn that pulses in time with the pipes. Grins with all his teeth.
Shouts everything, because the music is loud and because he likes shouting. His hooves
are bleeding and he has not noticed.

## Personality & motivation
Joyful, competitive, incapable of stopping. He thinks today is the best day of his
life and he is correct, he just does not know why. He loves a challenge, a drink and
an audience. He would never give up the ruby for money. He would give it up to the
better dancer, or to anyone who could make a louder noise than his pipes, or to anyone
who could make the reel stop, which he does not believe is possible.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: "Dance!"; the ruby "fell out of the sky this morning, into my cup, so I wore it"; the casks "came off a cart" (he does not say when); that the goat path up the north-east shore reaches the ridge "if you have hooves"; on the lake: "It draws us! Look at the chin on me. Go on, skate a bit, see what it makes of you"
- Needs a dance-off won (three rounds, Performance or Acrobatics on ice DC 12/14/16): the ruby, with a bow and a hug
- Needs the reel stopped (the Morning-After, *silence*, or a louder noise: the castle's bell counts if anyone could arrange it): he sits down on the ice, looks at his hooves, and says "oh." Then he hands the ruby over without being asked and wants to know what day it is
