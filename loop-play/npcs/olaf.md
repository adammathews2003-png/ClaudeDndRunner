# Olaf

## Description
A frost giant the size of a barn lying across the floor of the deepest ice cave, one
hand clamped over a grey stone, snoring in a rhythm that is, if you listen, the
Midwinter reel at a quarter speed. His beard has icicles in it. There are three empty
monastery casks by his head. He mutters, in Giant, "never again," and once in a while
"the goose, the goose." The whole glacier breathes with him: sixty feet up, the cave
mouth narrows on his inhale and yawns on his exhale, and a town sets its clocks by it.

## Personality & motivation
Awake, he is a hungover frost giant who has been woken, and there is no personality to
speak of beyond that; it is all consequences. He crossed the Serpent's Stair in the
first snow, raided the monastery's cellar on his way through, drank it, found the one
cold stone in the kingdom, and lay down on it because it was the only thing that
helped. He wants to sleep. He would never give up the stone awake; asleep, he does
not know he is holding it.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: snoring
- He is not an authored encounter and the danger stone does not count him; he is the Hollow Glacier's hazard. Waking him: any failed check at the stone, a shout, a torch in his face, or Horace's honk. Woken, he fights for a round or two, then chases anything that runs, then goes back to sleep within the hour with his hand on the stone again
- Asleep, he can be worked around: the stone comes free with Sleight of Hand DC 15 (one try per minute; each failure is a wake check, Olaf's passive Perception 10 against the PC's Stealth), or a slow Strength job over ten minutes with a wake check at the end
- The reel rhythm in his snoring is the Dance's doing: he can be put deeper asleep by anyone playing it (Performance DC 12), which gives advantage on the next attempt
