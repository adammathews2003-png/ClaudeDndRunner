# King Aldous IV

## Description
Round, pink, sixty, in a fur robe he has clearly been looking forward to wearing. Laughs
first and loudest. Calls everyone "my dear fellow", including his daughter. Has had,
he will tell you, a marvellous day: the snow, the geese, the ale, and a goose-herder
crowned king, what a story.

## Personality & motivation
Generous, incurious, delighted by tradition because it means he does not have to decide
anything. He loves the Midwinter crowning as the one night a year he gets to be
somebody's subject. He would never cancel it. He would, if asked nicely by someone he
likes, "see what can be done", and then forget.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: the whole Midwinter programme; the lottery is "ancient and absolutely fair"; the Crown "has been in the family since before there was a family"; Hawksmoor's castle is "a wonderful thing, we must get him down for supper some time"; on the hat law: "Hats! We are a kingdom of hats, my dear fellow. Put one on and be somebody. I wore a cook's cap once, for a bet, and they had me doing eggs until Michaelmas"
- Needs persuasion (DC 12): the Crown's old rhyme (he had it from his nurse): "a day as king and a wish for the day, and at midnight the king is sent on his way"; he thinks it means bedtime
- Needs persuasion (DC 15): he will give a written order that the chapel guard admit the party, which gets them to the Crown before 20:45 (and the Crown gets down like a cat anyway)
