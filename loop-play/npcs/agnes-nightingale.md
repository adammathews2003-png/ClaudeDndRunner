# Agnes Nightingale

## Description
A brisk woman of thirty-five in a grey smock with a lamp hung from her stall's awning
whatever the hour, Mother Temperance's niece and the town's only apothecary. Labels
everything. Underlines the warnings. Looks at people the way a cook looks at a fish.

## Personality & motivation
Practical, kind in a way that feels like being told off, and quietly proud of her
brewing. She disapproves of the ale, the festival and the Crown, in that order, and
sells the cures for all three. She would never sell a philter without a lecture. She
will give a potion away to anyone bleeding, and charge them double for the next one.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: what each bottle does, exactly; that the monks' ale is "a toast you cannot stop and a headache you cannot cure, and I have tried"; that her aunt has the Midwinter song in the Hymnal, all nine verses
- Needs persuasion (DC 12): her shelf "restocks itself", which she has decided is a blessing and does not examine; she has sold the same potion of greater healing to the same guardsman four times today by her count, which is wrong, and she knows it is wrong
- Never: nothing secret; she is as hollow as the rest and sharper than most
