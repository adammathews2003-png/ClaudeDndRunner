# Captain Ingrid Bergmann

## Description
Forty, tall, grey-eyed, a scar through one eyebrow that she got from a goose and will
not discuss. Wears her mail under a festival tabard she plainly resents. Says little,
and all of it useful. Her patrol loves her and is terrified of her in equal measure.

## Personality & motivation
Dutiful, dry, unsentimental, with a sense of humour that surfaces about once a day,
usually at the King. She wants the wolves out, the gate shut, the drunks home and the
Princess accounted for. She would never leave her post. She would, for anyone who
helped at the gate, bend a rule, once.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: the wolves come down the Bell Road at dawn "every Midwinter, they smell the sausages"; the patrol musters at 06:45 and drives them off up the road; her advice to travellers is "stay indoors until the bell"; the chapel guard is hers and will not be bribed
- Freely shares, on the hat law, to anyone in a helmet: "If you're in a helmet you're on my rota. Take it off or take the gate." She means it: a PC in a guardsman's helmet is given a post (the chapel door, 12:00–19:30) and expected at it
- Needs persuasion (DC 12) or help at the gate: the patrol is slow because the night watch cannot be woken before 06:45; she has shouted at them, every Midwinter; she suspects the ale. If someone rings the alarm bell at the gate, the patrol comes in ten minutes instead
- Needs trust (ally): she has noticed that the wolves are the same three wolves, that the big one has the same torn ear as last Midwinter, and that this bothers her more than it should. She will, if convinced the day is wrong, stand her patrol down from the Hall at 21:00 so that nobody hollow gets hurt
