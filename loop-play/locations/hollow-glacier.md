# The Hollow Glacier

## Description
The glacier that fills the north end of the bowl is hollow underneath: blue ice caves
carved by a stream that does not run in winter, opening at the glacier's foot where the
Bell Road ends. Everything inside groans: the ice, the wind, and, increasingly as you go
deeper, the inhabitants. It is a miserable place. The yeti has a headache. The mephits
are crying. Something enormous in the deepest hall is snoring like a landslide and
muttering "never again" in Giant. The groaning is loudest at a dull grey stone frozen
into the floor under the snorer's hand.

The glacier breathes. Every ten minutes the cave mouth narrows to a crawl on the inhale
and yawns wide on the exhale, in time with the snoring from below; Bellwether sets its
clocks by "the glacier's second yawn" and has done for as long as anyone can remember
this morning. Go in on the yawn. Come out on the yawn. Nobody discusses the alternative.

## Areas
- **mouth** — the cave mouth at the road's end; wolf tracks in and out; blue light
- **weeping-gallery** — a long low gallery of icicles (z −10); ice mephits sob in the ceiling; the walls sweat
- **the-slide** — a smooth chute down (z −10 to −40); Acrobatics DC 12 or arrive prone and loud
- **yeti-den** — a round chamber at the slide's foot; bones, a nest, and a yeti holding its head
- **the-cold-hall** — the deepest cavern (z −60); Olaf the frost giant asleep across the whole floor, one hand over the stone; a bricked arch in the south wall leads up toward the Keep's cellars (two hours' crawl)

## Items & features
- The Morning-After (piece; under Olaf's left hand, frozen into the floor): a dull grey stone, cold enough to hurt. Whoever holds it is immune to being charmed by cheer, revelry or drink, and can end any toast, song or dance in earshot by saying "right, that's enough" (WIS save DC 13 to keep going). At the ceremony, pressed into the Crown last, it sobers the Crown so it will hear a new wish. Once carried, the regenerated copy is `duplicate; hollow` (a pebble; Olaf sleeps just as badly on it)
- Olaf's sack (cold-hall): three empty Patient Brewers' casks, a monk's sandal, and a pass-marker from the Serpent's Stair
- The yeti's nest (yeti-den): a dented pot of monastery ale, cold; the yeti is drinking it for the headache and it is not helping
- The bricked arch (cold-hall, south): mortar from the Keep's side; loose enough to work free in an hour (STR DC 15 or tools)

## Hidden
- DC 12 (mouth): among the wolf tracks, one set of goose prints, going in
- DC 13 (weeping-gallery): the mephits' tears have frozen into a path along the floor; following it avoids the slide
- DC 15 (cold-hall): Olaf's hand is not resting on the stone, it is gripping it; he is holding on to it in his sleep
- DC 17 (cold-hall): the snoring has a rhythm, and the rhythm is the Midwinter reel, slowed down

## Notes / current state
Sidekick clause (Biscuit): in the weeping gallery the post-marmot puts his nose down
and follows the mephits' frozen tears by smell, round the slide and down the tear-path
to the den's back way, no check; he sorts letters by smell and tears are, he feels, a
kind of letter. Without him: DC 13 Perception to find the tear-path, or take the slide
(Acrobatics DC 12; a failure arrives prone and loud in the yeti den and "the cave of
groans" is on at once); either way the yeti is awake and the quiet way to the cold hall
is gone for the day. Biscuit will not go into the cold hall: the snoring is the one
thing he is afraid of, and he sits at the arch and shivers until carried.

Olaf is not an authored encounter: he is a sleeping frost giant (npcs/olaf.md) and a
consequence. Prying the stone out from under his hand is Sleight of Hand DC 15 or a
slow Strength job, each with a chance of waking him (he wakes on any failed check, a
shout, or a goose). A woken Olaf is CR 8 and furious, and the right answer is to run;
a party that does not run will wake up at the inn. He goes back to sleep within the
hour, hand back on the stone.
