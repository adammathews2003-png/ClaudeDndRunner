# The Market Square

## Description
Bellwether's heart: a broad cobbled square under the bell tower, ringed by shuttered
stalls and festival bunting, snow trodden to brown slush by the morning crowd. The
lottery barrel stands on a cart by the bell tower's foot, with Bastable's clipboard
beside it. A straw Winter King on a pole watches over everything. At ten the whole
town is here; at eight in the evening it empties up Keep Way behind the King.

## Areas
- **the-square** — open ground; stalls round the edge (hot wine, roast chestnuts, paper crowns)
- **bell-tower** — the Great Bell in its open tower at the center; the stair is locked (Bergmann has the key); it has not been rung today
- **lottery-cart** — by the tower's foot; the barrel of straws; 10:00
- **apothecary-stall** — east side of the square, under a green awning; Agnes Nightingale's Midwinter stall (08:00–19:00)

## Items & features
- The lottery barrel: a hundred straws, one long one; everyone draws; the King reads the straw. Horace eats the losers
- The Great Bell: cast with the Midwinter rhyme round its lip; rings midnight every year, famously; has never been heard to do so by anyone the party will meet
- The straw Winter King (on a pole): a paper crown with a goose drawn on it
- The hat stall (the-square): paper crowns 1 cp, felt caps 2 cp, a cook's cap 3 cp, a guardsman's helmet "for dressing up" 5 sp; under the hat law a purchase here is a change of career, and Nance the hatter asks "and what will you be?" as she takes the coin, and writes it down

## Hidden
- DC 12 (lottery-cart): the long straw is already damp before it is drawn, as if from a hand that has held it many times
- DC 14 (bell-tower): the bell's clapper is tied up with a goose-down cord nobody admits to
