# Widow's Ridge Signal Tower

## Description
A bare ridge above the glacier where the wind never stops, with an old square signal
tower on its crest, roofless, its beacon-iron long gone. From here the whole kingdom
lies below like a bowl of milk. Two gargoyles squat on the tower's corners, which the
locals say are statues and which are not. At about two in the afternoon, Castle Lark
comes round on its circuit and passes so low over the ridge that its rope ladder drags
across the tower's parapet. Anyone standing on the parapet can grab it. Anyone standing
anywhere else can watch.

## Areas
- **trail-head** — where the ridge trail and the goat path from the lake meet; a cairn
- **tower-foot** — the tower's base; a door that no longer exists; rubble
- **tower-top** — the parapet (z 40); a broken stair gets most of the way up (Athletics DC 10 for the last 8 ft); the ladder passes at 14:00

## Items & features
- The cairn (trail-head): someone has stacked a great many small stones on it and written "NOT TUESDAY" on the top one in chalk
- The parapet (tower-top): wide enough to stand on; the wind is a problem (DEX save DC 10 when the ladder comes, or take 1d6 and be prone, which is fine, the ladder comes back tomorrow)
- The ladder (14:00–14:10): a rope ladder 60 ft long dangling from the castle's gatehouse; climbing it is Athletics DC 12 while the castle moves on; failure is a fall of 10–30 ft onto the ridge, not into the valley, because Hawksmoor was an architect and planned for it

## Hidden
- DC 13 (tower-foot): the gargoyles have shifted position since you first looked at them
- DC 15 (tower-top): scratched into the parapet: "2PM. LADDER. DON'T LET THE GOOSE ON."
