# The Monastery of the Patient Brewers

## Description
A long stone cloister on the south-west slope, an hour's walk up the Brewers' Track,
with copper stills steaming through the snow. The monks of the Patient Order brew the
best ale in three kingdoms and never hurry anything. The cloister smells of malt and
wet wool. In the refectory a toast is in progress; it has been in progress since
breakfast and nobody can quite bring it to a close. Abbot Bede presides, benignly, over
a monastery that is slowly getting drunk.

## Areas
- **gate** — the gatehouse on the track; a bell-pull and a sign ("Please wait. We will get to you.")
- **cloister** — the square garden, snowed in; the stills along the south side
- **refectory** — long hall off the cloister's north side; the Great Toast runs here
- **brewhouse** — the mash tuns; where the amber stone fell in on Day 1
- **cellar** — stairs from the brewhouse (z −15); the casks; Brother Grunt's straw bed in the far vault

## Items & features
- The Toast (piece; carried by Brother Grunt, in his stomach): an amber stone the size of a walnut, warm, faintly fizzing. Whoever holds it cannot end a toast badly and cannot be interrupted while speaking one; at the ceremony it makes the Knights of Nothing raise their cups and stand down. Once carried, the regenerated copy is `duplicate; hollow` (a glass bead that tastes of nothing)
- Midwinter Ale: a cask of it to anyone who wins the Great Toast (18:00); a toast made over it runs long and ends in applause
- The Great Toast (refectory, 18:00): a contest of speech and stamina. Three rounds: Charisma (Performance) DC 12, then DC 14 with a heckler, then a Constitution save DC 13 against the ale; the winner gets the cask and the Abbot's favour
- Brother Grunt's bowl (cellar): a wooden trough; he is fed at 08:00, 13:00 and 19:00

## Hidden
- DC 12 (brewhouse): a monk's note on the tun: "found a stone in the mash, Day 1, gave it to Grunt for safekeeping. He ate it. — Br. Pell"
- DC 14 (refectory): the toast being made is the same toast, word for word, every time it comes round to Brother Pell
- DC 15 (cellar): Grunt's stomach fizzes audibly, and he burps small amber sparks
- DC 12 (gate): under the sign "Please wait. We will get to you." there is a smaller sign, "We have not forgotten you. — The Order", and under that, smaller still, "Still here? Good."
