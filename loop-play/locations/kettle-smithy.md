# Kettle's Smithy

## Description
A squat stone forge on the lane between the square and the Weather Tower, roof steaming
where the snow meets the chimney heat. The shop front is one shutter, propped open,
with blades racked behind it and a tray of crampons on the sill. Hesper Kettle works
at the anvil in the back and does not look up when the bell rings. A sign over the
door: "NO CREDIT, NO HAGGLING, NO GEESE". The Goose-Cleaver hangs in the window.

## Areas
- **shop** — the shuttered front; the racks, the sill of crampons, a counter with a slot for coin
- **forge** — behind the counter; anvil, bellows, quench trough; warm enough to dry a wet party in ten minutes
- **loft** — ladder from the forge; Hesper's bed and the coal

## Items & features
- The Goose-Cleaver (shop window): a +1 handaxe (uncommon), 350 gp; see Hesper's Stock; made for Captain Bergmann, who refused it on principle
- The quench trough (forge): warm water; a dropped coin in it is still there at 06:00 the next loop, which Hesper has not noticed
- The crampon tray (shop): six pairs; the single most useful purchase in Caldermont today

## Hidden
- DC 12 (shop): the dust on the window-blade's edge has never been disturbed; it was hung this morning and has hung there a very long time
