# The Sleeping Marmot

## Description
A fat, low-beamed coaching inn by the South Gate, smelling of pine smoke, wet wool and
the famous monastery ale. The common room has a hearth the size of a cart, a bar made
from a single pass-timber, and a stuffed marmot over the door that someone has crowned
with paper. Upstairs is a long loft of beds under the roof slope, where stranded
travellers sleep three to a row. The innkeeper, Dotty Hargreave, is already shouting at
the kitchen when the party wakes. The inn is also the post office: the kingdom's letters
go by trained marmots that tunnel under the snow, and a brass hatch by the hearth is
where they come up, shake, and hand over the post. At 06:02 one of them, Biscuit, climbs
the stairs with a letter for the loft. Pim, the boot-boy, is already up and running.

## Areas
- **loft** — top of the stairs; one long room under the roof (z 15); the party's four beds are the row nearest the gable window, which looks north toward the Keep. The loop-bed.
- **common-room** — front door from South Street; the hub; stairs up in the west wall, kitchen door east
- **kitchen** — door in the common room's east wall; Dotty's domain
- **cellar** — hatch in the kitchen floor (z −10); ale casks; cold and dry
- **stable-yard** — side door from the kitchen; gate onto South Street; the sausage cart's overnight berth (empty by 06:00)

## Items & features
- The party's beds (loft, gable row): whatever a PC puts under the mattress is gone at 06:00; whatever they are holding or wearing is not
- The post-hatch (common-room, by the hearth): brass, warm, marmot-sized; the post-marmots come up through it, and Dotty's rule is "don't feed them, they unionise"
- The 06:02 letter (loft, delivered by Biscuit to Row Four): "To the Occupant. PLEASE DO NOT GO TO THE GATE. Sincerely, W." It is there every morning; in loop 1 it is the second thing Wil asks about ("did you get my letter? Nobody reads the letter")
- Pim's boots (kitchen door): a boot-boy's row of guests' boots, polished; a cook's cap hangs on the peg above them, which under the hat law makes him the Keep's problem on feast days
- Chalk tally on the loft's gable beam: a few hundred strokes in a dozen different hands, then "STOPPED COUNTING. SORRY. W." (it is there every morning; nobody at the inn knows who W. is)
- Notice board (common-room): the Midwinter programme (lottery 10:00 in the square; procession 20:00; crowning 21:00 at the Keep; "all welcome, bring a cup")
- Bar: a cask of Patient Brewers' Midwinter Ale, tapped; a toast made over it tends to go on a bit
- Dotty's ledger: four names, "paid through Day 1, caravan turned back at Hob's Rest"

## Hidden
- DC 13 (loft): under the fourth bed, a goose feather and a very old boiled sweet
- DC 15 (common-room): the paper crown on the stuffed marmot has a goose drawn on it in a careful hand
- DC 14 (common-room): the post-marmots sort the letters by sniffing them, and the one that does the loft's row sniffs the party's letter every morning and sighs
