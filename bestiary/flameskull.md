# Flameskull

## Description
A human skull wreathed in green flame, floating at head height and turning to face whoever
speaks. The fire throws no heat until it wants to. It talks, usually too much, in the
voice it had when it had a throat.

## Traits
- **Illumination.** The flameskull sheds bright light in a 15-foot radius and dim light for another 15 feet. It can douse or rekindle the light as a bonus action.
- **Magic Resistance.** The flameskull has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
- **Rejuvenation.** If destroyed, the flameskull re-forms from its scattered bones in 1 hour, unless the bones are sprinkled with holy water or the binding that holds it is broken.

## Attacks
| name | hit | damage | range | notes |
|------|-----|--------|-------|-------|
| fire ray | +5 | 3d6 fire | 30/30 | a line of green flame from the eye sockets |

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** The flameskull makes two fire ray attacks.
- **Flame Burst (Recharge 5–6).** Green fire blooms in a 20-foot-radius sphere centred on a point the flameskull can see within 60 feet. Each creature in it must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 17 (5d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

## Reactions
(none)

## Backstory
A flameskull is what is left of a wizard who bound themselves to their own bones rather than
die with work unfinished. The working keeps the mind and burns away almost everything else:
appetite, sleep, patience, the ability to let an argument go. They guard what they made, or
what they were made to guard, and most of them would rather talk than fight. Most of them
also have no say in the matter.
