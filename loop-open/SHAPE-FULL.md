## Premise as the players hear it
The party are caravan guards snowed into Kettlebrook, capital of the tiny mountain
kingdom of Branholt; every pass is shut. Their employer, cheese merchant Dolorous Pike,
insists the south pass "isn't that bad." Midwinter morning they wake in the Stag &
Kettle's loft with a rope round their ankles and a hungover stranger asleep at the other
end of it. They die that day. Then they wake in the same beds on the same morning with
the stranger, Phil, at the foot of the bed holding porridge and a glowing pebble. The
day repeats; a ceremony in the keep's observatory at nine tonight is what does it; it
will kill them if they walk in; four things in the kingdom might help; the pebble says
which places will kill them. He isn't coming.

## Opening
Day 1 06:00, loop 0, the loft. Phil leaves at 06:10 ("Good. It worked, probably. Go

## The truth (GM)
The Society of the Still Hour (Astronomer-Royal Isidore Brahe, Fellows Tibbs and Pook,
and the founder's skull Ptolemy, a flameskull) built the Orrery of Hours in the Royal
Observatory. On Midwinter night they perform the Vigil: at midnight, with the Still Star
in the great lens and the Great Bell unstruck (its clapper was sent to Castle Aloft "for
re-tuning" on Day -40), the Orrery folds the day back to 06:00. Whatever is inside the
ring at the fold is fixed outside time: memory and possessions. Brahe reads his
instruments backwards, so the Society stood outside the ring and got nothing. The one
man inside it was Philbert Connery, a sellsword hired by Dame Shackleton on Day -1 to
stop the Vigil, who dove into the ring to escape Ptolemy's fireball. ~10,957 loops,
thirty years. Nobody else remembers; the Society does it every night thinking it's the
first time. Phil is the working's anchor (a fold with nothing fixed inside burns out by
dawn); he doesn't know; Ptolemy does.

## The looper's secret
He was running, not fighting, when he fell in. He has never told Shackleton he failed;
she still expects his report by noon, every day, and he avoids the ice because of her.
He delivers the mission in her words because he's lost his own. He's known for ten
thousand loops that contact with a fixed person at the fold fixes you, and only now used
it (the rope, the fold before loop 0), out of shame. He says the goat remembers. It
doesn't.

## Stopping it, the twist, the ending
Three paths: strike midnight (the Clapper back in Old Patience); plant the Mirror in the
lens cradle so the Orrery over-winds and collapses without a fight; or enter the Vigil
with the King's Key and turn it three times at the hub from 23:30 while fighting the
fixed encounter. Twist: whoever is inside the ring at an unbroken fold becomes the next
anchor; the Almanac's margin and Ptolemy warn, Phil can't. Ending: any unfolded midnight
ends the loop; Day 2 dawns; Phil sleeps for the first time in thirty years; Brahe, if
alive, flees up the chain; the passes are still shut and Gerald still at the Notch
(sequel hooks). Consumables regenerate hollow; coin regenerates.

   L3, yellow L4, green L5.

   firepower is Ptolemy; 4,400 sits on the deadly boundary, hence yellow at L5.
3. Stone vs builder tension (system-level): `threat.xp` is fixed, `encounter build`
   rescales by word. Resolved in planning/07 (2026-10-04): the word is pinned to
   `start-level`; `build` scales count for party size and item power, not level.
4. Looper + villain + 12 others; the two Fellows share one file.
5. Party-location at the opening is the inn loft; Layouts are on the Notch yard and
   the dome.
6. Outside sites use `parent: world` (sites directly in a mi frame). The `sky-ferry`
   route uses `kind: chain` with a time override.
7. Three "non-encounters" (Dottie, the Fellows at home, the mule) are prose, not
   ENCOUNTER lines.
