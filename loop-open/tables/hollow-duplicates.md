# Hollow duplicates — what a regenerated copy of a carried thing is like
<!-- When the party return to where a carried piece (or a magic item they already took) came from, the world's copy is
     there and inert (`notes: duplicate; hollow`). Roll 1d10 or pick; comedy is allowed, breaking the fiction is not. -->

| roll | result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | The second King's Key opens nothing. It is warm, as if it had been in a pocket, and it hums very faintly, out of tune |
| 2 | The duplicate Clapper is iron to the eye and the hand, and when struck rings like a wet sock |
| 3 | The Mirror's copy shows the room one second late. Anselm finds this restful |
| 4 | The Almanac's copy has pages, all of them the same page, and the page says "see previous page" |
| 5 | The Wheel of Heroism grows back and is cheese. Excellent cheese. Just cheese |
| 6 | A potion already drunk on an earlier loop regenerates as water with a faint rumor of raspberry |
| 7 | The duplicate longbow from the Notch armory is fine, actually, but every arrow from it lands exactly where the last one did |
| 8 | Pike, regenerated, remembers nothing, still owes the party 60 gp, and denies it with fresh conviction |
| 9 | Baaldur the goat looks at the party as if *they* were the duplicates |
| 10 | Clerk Tallow, if the Key was taken yesterday, has a second chain round his neck with nothing on it, and the audit is two lines short |
