# Ottoline Brask

## Description
A square woman with forearms like hams, an apron scorched to lace, and a daughter (Pell,
twelve, terrifying) who does the sums. The shop is the forge: racks of honest steel, a wall
of shields, one glass case nobody leans on. Prices are chalked on the wall, never rubbed out.

## Personality & motivation
Blunt, fair, immovable on price ("it's on the wall"). Wants the Midwinter trade and the
Society's custom, which she has never had because "they want everything in brass."
Would never sell a weapon to anyone who calls it a toy. Would never haggle; Pell might,
for a story about the flying castle.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: what everything on the wall does; that the Notch garrison bought six winter cloaks from her in autumn and "never came back for the sharpening"
- Needs persuasion (DC 12): she made the brass plate on the bell tower door for the Society, on Day -39, "and the screws were bad, which I said"
