# Marigold Gudgeon

## Description
Seventeen, freckled, loud, with a lute she is slowly learning and a voice she has
already finished. Runs the inn in practice. Knows everyone in town by what they
drink. Has decided the party are interesting and will not be moved from this.

## Personality & motivation
Bright, nosy, generous, immune to discouragement. Wants out of Kettlebrook and into
a story; failing that, wants a story to come to Kettlebrook. Would never betray a
confidence, but will set it to music. Her song at 18:00 is a ballad of the kingdom's
history that is wrong in every particular and correct about Brahe's nose.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: who is where in town at what hour (she is the GM's shortcut to every NPC's Movements, told as gossip); that the Fellows polish the Orrery in the afternoons; that Dottie the ringer airs her knitting out of the belfry louvre
- Needs a kind word (DC 10): she has seen Phil cry on the sledge at midnight "like he knew something was ending, and then it didn't"
- Needs persuasion (DC 12): a verse she wrote about the night the clapper went, which names "the brass-nosed man and the castle in the sky" and is, by accident, accurate

- 09:00–12:00 → kettlebrook (the market hall; gossip exchange)

- 18:00–18:30 → stag-and-kettle/common-room (the song)
- 18:30–24:00 → stag-and-kettle/common-room (service; counts the till at 23:00)
Will carry a message anywhere in town for a copper and a verse.
