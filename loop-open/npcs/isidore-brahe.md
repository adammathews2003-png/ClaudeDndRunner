# Astronomer-Royal Isidore Brahe

## Description
Tall, stooped, magnificent in blue velvet, with a brass nose held on by a ribbon, which
he polishes in conversation. Speaks in the grand manner of a man who has rehearsed
being remembered. Vain, precise, and quite incapable of noticing that every instrument
he owns reads backwards to him, because he had them all made to his own drawings.
Carries a hatbox everywhere after 20:30 and speaks to it as to a superior.

## Personality & motivation
Wants immortality for himself and his Fellows, tonight, through the Vigil: the Society
inside the Still Hour forever, the kingdom as their clock. Believes it is Midwinter
night and that he is about to succeed. Courteous to a fault until crossed; then
outraged, then dangerous. Fears being wrong more than death. Would never harm the King
(he needs the keep) and will not strike first in public. In the dome he will.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares (at length): the Still Star, the Orrery's beauty, the Vigil as a "purely scientific observance"; that outsiders are not welcome in the keep after 20:00
- Needs persuasion (DC 14) or flattery: that the Clapper is with Vexilla "for re-tuning" at his request; that Shackleton was expelled "for insubordination" and took "a notebook"
- Only under duress / never: what the Vigil does; that the ring fixes what is inside it; that he has stood outside it (he does not know this is a mistake)
- Does not know: that it has happened 10,957 times; that his diagram is mirrored (Ptolemy has told him; he did not listen); that the Working has an anchor and that it is a lodger at the Stag & Kettle
