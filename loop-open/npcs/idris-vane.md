# Captain Idris Vane

## Description
Broad, scarred, grey at the temples, in a breastplate she has worn so long it has her
shape. Carries the keep's bunch of keys on a chain and jingles when she thinks. Says
little, and that slowly, and means it.

## Personality & motivation
Dutiful, suspicious, fair. Wants Midwinter to pass without anyone dying, which she has
achieved every year so far by shutting the gate on time. Dislikes the Society's hired
watch on the bell tower, dislikes the Fellows in the dome, dislikes Brahe's lock on
the tower stair, and has been overruled on all three. Would never disobey a signed
royal order. Would never let her guards be killed for a stranger's story. Can be made
an ally by evidence (the Society's ledger, Brahe's letter) and a plan that keeps her
people out of it.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: the gate shuts at 20:00; outsiders are questioned in the square at 15:00; the Notch garrison is "overdue a report, which happens"
- Needs persuasion (DC 12): the Society's watch at the bell tower is "irregular"; the Star Tower stair has had a new lock since Day -40 and she does not have a key to it
- Needs evidence or DC 15: she will let a party with a royal order through at any hour; she will not go up the stair herself ("it's astronomy, by order"); if shown the Society is paying for a watch to keep the bell silent she "has questions for the Astronomer-Royal in the morning," which in this kingdom is never
