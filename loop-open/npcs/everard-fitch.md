# Chamberlain Everard Fitch

## Description
Small, immaculate, exhausted; a chain of office he polishes with his sleeve, a sheaf
of lists, and the face of a man who has been running a kingdom on behalf of someone
who is building a model of it out of cheese. Speaks quickly, in sub-clauses. Can be
found wherever the King is not, fixing what the King did.

## Personality & motivation
Loyal, competent, resentful in private. Wants the Midwinter feast (19:00, the Great
Hall) to go off without the King mentioning the cheese model to the Society. Regards
Brahe as a pompous bore the King is fond of and the Vigil as "astronomy, I'm told."
Would never let outsiders into the keep without a reason, and will accept almost any
reason that comes with a bow. Would never cross Captain Vane in front of the guard.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: the keep is closed after 20:00 "for the Vigil, at the Astronomer-Royal's request, signed by the King, who signs things"; the King receives petitioners at 11:00 and will sign anything put in front of him with a pen
- Needs persuasion (DC 12): a royal order (signed at 11:00) gets its bearer through the gate at any hour; the King's Key "went to the Mint eighty years ago and they lost it, we've been using Vane's bunch since"
- Needs persuasion (DC 14): the Society pays Sergeant Ambry's watch out of the Society's purse, not the Crown's, "which is irregular, and I have said so, in a memorandum"
