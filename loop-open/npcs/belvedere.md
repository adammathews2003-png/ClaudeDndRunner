# Belvedere

## Description
A gargoyle in a butler's apron, grey, horned, immaculate, with a feather duster in
one claw and a notebook in the other. Speaks in a low, patient, carrying voice and
addresses everyone as "sir or madam." Counts the teacups at 14:00. Has an under-butler,
Stanwick, also a gargoyle, who says nothing and carries trays.

## Personality & motivation
Correct, lonely, proud of the house. Wants nothing moved, nothing stolen and nobody
rude; wants, underneath, for someone to admire the silver. Will hand over anything in
the castle against proper paperwork (a signed note from Brahe or Vexilla; a forgery at
DC 14 Deception; a Society signet will do with a straight face), and will fight to the
death over anything taken without it. Would never harm a guest who has wiped their
feet. Would never let the rug eat anyone who was polite to it.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: the mistress is in the lowlands "until the spring, or the summer, or when she remembers"; the basket runs at 13:00 and 13:30; the Clapper is "on the bench, on loan, not available"; the house rules (feet, hats, no whistling)
- Needs persuasion (DC 12) or a compliment about the silver: Brahe's letter asked the mistress "to keep the tongue until the Star has passed," and she laughed at it and put it on the bench and left; Belvedere does not know what it means and does not approve of laughing at the Astronomer-Royal, who tips
- Needs persuasion (DC 15): the castle's garden looks down on the Observatory dome; on the evening of Day -1 he watched the Fellows rehearse by lamplight, "all of them standing outside the ring, which seemed the wrong side for a ring, sir or madam," and he has put this in the notebook under Irregularities
