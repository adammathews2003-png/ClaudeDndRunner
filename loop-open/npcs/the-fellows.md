# Fellows Tibbs and Pook

## Description
Tibbs is short, round and anxious; Pook is tall, thin and anxious. Both wear the blue
robe and the brass star pin, both carry notebooks they never open, and both finish the
other's sentences with the wrong ending. They are the Society's entire junior
membership. Pook is in charge of the hatbox. Tibbs is in charge of Pook.

## Personality & motivation
Devoted, useless, fundamentally decent. Want Brahe to be pleased, which has never
happened. Will do anything for a kind word. Pook secretly wants to be a cartographer
and misses Dame Shackleton, who was nice to him once. Tibbs secretly wants lunch. They
are noncombatants: in the dome they chant and hold the Working's rhythm, and a loud
noise (or a hand on either shoulder) breaks it. Cornered, they surrender immediately and
apologise for being in the way.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely share: the Vigil's timetable as a list of chores ("lens at eleven, Grandmaster on the plinth, cocoa at half past"); that the keep is shut from 20:00; that the Astronomer-Royal is "a genius, probably"
- Needs persuasion (DC 10) or any kindness: the Clapper went to the flying castle; Dame Shackleton took "the blue book"; the Wardens let in anyone with a pin
- Needs persuasion (DC 13): where the spare signet is; that Brahe "doesn't let us inside the ring, which is a shame, it looks warm"
- Never: they do not know what the Vigil does; they think it is an observance
