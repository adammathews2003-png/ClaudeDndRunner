# Hespera Lind

## Description
Tall, grey, exact, in a stained linen coat with forty pockets and a pencil behind each
ear. Her shop is a wall of jars with handwritten labels and a counter you are not to
touch. Smells of camphor, honey and something that was once a frog. Speaks as if
reading from the label.

## Personality & motivation
Precise, kind under it, allergic to nonsense and to Wendel Quist. Wants nobody to die of
anything she could have prevented, and keeps a ledger of who bought what in case they
do. Would never sell a thing she hasn't tested. Would never say what is in the kettle
grog, because she tested it, and was unwell.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: what every jar does; that the Fellows buy "nose polish" weekly, "which is lamp oil, with a label"; that Dottie's grog "aids climbing and nothing else"
- Needs persuasion (DC 12): Brahe bought "every drop of cold-resistance I had" on Day -31, the day before he went up the Shoulder and came back without what he went for
- Needs persuasion (DC 14): she has treated Phil for frostbite "more times than the ledger will hold, and always the same frostbite"
