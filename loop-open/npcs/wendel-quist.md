# Wendel Quist

## Description
A narrow, delighted man in a coat of many pockets, each with a different smell, and a
tray on a strap that unfolds into a shop. Everything he sells is "genuine," "the last
one," and "from the castle, probably." Some of it is. By day a rug and the tray in the
market hall; by evening the tray on a corner table at the Stag & Kettle.

## Personality & motivation
Cheerful, inventive, honest about the price and nothing else. Wants to be the man who
sold the heroes the thing that did it. Would never sell the same person the same dud
twice ("professional pride"). Buys anything with a story, at a quarter, and resells it with a better one.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: every rumor on tables/rumors.md, with embellishments; that the Sky Ferry basket "takes passengers if you're in it when it goes"; that the Mint's coins "come up on the Spur after a thaw"
- Needs persuasion (DC 12): he bought a brass nose from a Fellow on Day -5 "and sold it to the castle's basket, which is a customer, in its way"
- Needs persuasion (DC 14): Phil sold him "a green pebble, years ago, and bought it back the next morning for twice the price, and did that for a month, and I've never had a better customer"
