# Grandmaster Ptolemy

## Description
A yellowed skull with green fire where its eyes were, in a hatbox lined with blue silk,
which it finds beneath it. Three hundred years dead; the Society's founder; bound to its
own bones by a working it describes as "my best, frankly." Voice like a lecturer who
has lost the thread and blames the students. Lid up, it talks; lid down, it sulks.

## Personality & motivation
Pedantic, lonely, delighted by anyone who asks a question that is not about star
angles. Despises Brahe's diagram and has said so for forty years. Bound to defend the
Society and to take part in the Vigil whether it likes it or not; it does not like it.
Wants to be proven right, which is a thing the party can offer. Would never lie about
astronomy. Lies freely about everything else for fun, then admits it.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: the Still Star's motion to the minute; that the Vigil is at 21:00 and "something rather drastic" at midnight; that Brahe's diagram is mirrored and the Society stands "on the wrong side of their own ring, which is funnier than you think"
- Needs persuasion (DC 12) or a good question: the Working fixes whatever is inside the ring at the fold; three turns of a key at the hub unwinds it; a struck midnight breaks the fold; the Star reflected back over-winds it
- Needs a bribe (a new hat, a better box) or DC 16: that a Working like this needs an anchor, something fixed, or it burns out by dawn; that there must therefore already be one, and that if the party stop the fold with someone standing inside the ring, that someone is the next anchor; "step out before the last stroke; I'd have told the sellsword that too, if he'd asked"
- Never: it will not refuse to fight in the dome ("I'm bound; it's not personal; I'll aim high")
