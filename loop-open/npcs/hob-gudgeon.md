# Hob Gudgeon

## Description
A wide, soft, worried man in an apron that was white under a previous king. Bald,
sweating in all weathers, forever wiping something. Calls everyone "my duck." Has
never left Kettlebrook and regards the passes as a rumor.

## Personality & motivation
Kind, cowardly, a terrible businessman. Wants a quiet inn and for Marigold to stop
singing about him. Lets Phil live in the cupboard because Phil once (Day -200, he
thinks) fixed the hearth, and because Phil pays in exact coppers and never argues. Would
never turn a guest out in winter. Would never, ever tell the Society anything, out of a
general fear of brass.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: everything on tables/rumors.md, at a rate of three per drink; that Pike has not paid his bill; that the Society "don't come in here, thank the Thaw"
- Needs persuasion (DC 10): Phil has lived in the cupboard "longer than I can work out"; some mornings Phil knows what Hob is going to say and says it first, "which is a trick, I suppose"
- Needs persuasion (DC 14): the cellar's kettle grog is Marigold's; nobody knows what's in it; Dottie the ringer drinks it and says it helps her climb
