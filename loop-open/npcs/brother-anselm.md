# Brother Anselm

## Description
Tiny, ancient, wrapped in forty years of patched wool, with a white beard he has
plaited to keep it out of the soup and eyes that have been looking at one star for a
long time. Hums. Mishears on purpose. Is accompanied everywhere by Toby, an ogre in a
habit made of three habits, who holds up boards (npcs: statblock ogre; see
locations/glacier-shrine.md).

## Personality & motivation
Gentle, cracked, hospitable within reason. Believes the Still Star is a thing to be
watched, not used; refused Brahe the Mirror on Day -30 and still shakes about it.
Wants cheese, company, and for the Society to leave the sky alone. Would never give the
Mirror to anyone who wants to use it for the Working; will give it to anyone who swears
to use it against the Working, because that is still watching, just with intent. Would
never let Toby be hurt. Toby would never let Anselm be hurt, and has the boards to say so.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: the Star-faith's astronomy (the Still Star stands over the keep at midnight tonight and "will not move on until it is let"); that the Society "stole the mathematics and left the manners"; soup
- Needs a cheese or persuasion (DC 15): the Mirror; the Mirror "returns the Star's gaze," and a thing that looks at itself "unwinds"; the Society's lens "opens its eye at eleven"
- Needs persuasion (DC 12): Brahe came on Day -30 with the Fellows and a cart and "a face like a sum," and Toby held up NO until they left
- Does not know: anything about the loop; he has lived every day the same for forty years and would not notice one more
