# Dottie Varrow

## Description
Sixty-five, small and knotted, with forearms like a stevedore's from forty years on
the ropes and a scarf forty feet long and growing. Climbs 180 steps every morning with a
basket of kettle grog and knitting, airs the scarf out of the louvre, and comes down at
dark. Nobody has told her the tower is closed, because the brass plate is on the
outside and she goes in through the window.

## Personality & motivation
Patient, deaf in one ear, rude in the way of people who have earned it. Wants the
bell to ring; it has not since Day -40 and she regards this as a personal matter
between her and the Astronomer-Royal. Will help anyone who brings the Clapper, up to
and including swinging Old Patience herself at midnight. Would never leave the tower
during a ringing. Would never ring early: "midnight's midnight."

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: the clapper went "for re-tuning, which a bell doesn't need, I asked"; Sergeant Ambry's watch stands at the door from ten at night "paid by the brass-nose, not the Crown, I asked that too"; the louvre on the square side opens and the ivy holds a weight
- Needs a drink or persuasion (DC 10): the ringing-room's rope wheels still work; with the clapper seated, two people can ring from there without being in the belfry; the stroke takes a count of three and "the whole kingdom hears it, and so does whatever's listening up on that tower"
- Needs persuasion (DC 13): she saw, from the louvre on Day -40, Brahe's cart and "a basket on a chain come down out of the sky for the tongue of my bell"
