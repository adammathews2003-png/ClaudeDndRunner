# Mother Grubb

## Description
Enormous, immovable, enthroned in a chair the size of a bed with a wheel of cheese
on each arm, a knife in her belt and a goat on her feet for warmth. Laughs from
somewhere deep. Smells, powerfully, of the kingdom's one export. Has not stood up in
front of anyone since the coronation and does not intend to.

## Personality & motivation
Shrewd, generous, incurably curious about everyone else's business. Wants the rats
gone and the news brought. Feeds everyone who comes in, and prices information in
cheese. Would never cheat a customer; would never tell you the price before you've
eaten. Fond of Phil ("he talks to my goat; the goat doesn't mind") and of nobody in
the Society.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: the rats came up "from the back, where the draught is, and the draught smells like money"; everyone's cheese order (the Society takes a wheel a week; the castle's basket takes two; Anselm has had none since autumn and "it's a crime")
- Needs a rumor in trade: the Wheel of Heroism's wedges "put a fire in you for an hour; don't eat two"; that Baaldur the billy was a Marchwarden's goat and "wore the stone on his collar, the green one, till Phil had it off him for a song"
- Needs persuasion (DC 12): Brahe sent the Fellows to buy "every mirror in the hollow" on Day -29, and bought none, "there being no mirrors in a cheese cave, I told them"
