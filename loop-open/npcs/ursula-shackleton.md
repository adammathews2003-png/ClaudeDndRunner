# Dame Ursula Shackleton

## Description
Sixty, square, red-faced from the ice, in furs and a knitted cap with a brass
Marchwarden's badge pinned to it. Walks like a door opening. Speaks in short
declarative sentences and treats every setback as a measurement. Her wolves adore her.
Nobody else is permitted to.

## Personality & motivation
Stubborn, honest, furious. Called Brahe's diagram backwards in open session, was
expelled, and took his working notes on the way out because "evidence should be with
someone who can read it." Has tried the south pass forty mornings running (every one of
them the same morning, which she does not know). Wants to be proven right about Brahe,
and to get out of this kingdom, in that order. Would never give up an attempt before
the turning-back point. Would never lose at chess on purpose.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares: the passes are shut; Brahe is a fraud with a brass nose; the Society is "a lodge for men who like being lit from below"; the pass attempts, in detail
- Needs persuasion (DC 13), a chess win, or a plan she hasn't tried: she has the Almanac; she hired a sellsword on Day -1, "a quick man called Connery, half paid, lodging at the Stag," to stop the Vigil tonight, and expects him to report by noon; he never does (she does not know she has expected him 10,957 times, or that he avoids the ice because of her)
- Needs the party's live hazard stone shown to her: it was her grandfather's corps; she goes quiet; attitude → friendly; the Almanac is theirs
- Only under real pressure (DC 16): she suspects the Vigil "does something to time," from the Almanac's margins, and has been too afraid to be sure
