# Castle Aloft

## Description
The archmage Vexilla Bombast's castle hangs 2,000 ft above Lake Kettle on nothing at all,
a lump of grey stone with five towers, a garden, and a chain as thick as a man running
down to the Spindle on the west shore. Vexilla left for the lowlands in autumn and put the
castle "on hover"; her housekeeper, a gargoyle called Belvedere, runs it with the
furniture. Inside it is a very grand house in which nobody lives: fires laid and never
lit, a dining table set for twelve, armor that bows, a rug that follows you. The
workshop has the Great Bell's clapper on a bench with a label on it. The wind never stops.

**Reaching it:** the Sky Ferry, a winched basket on the mooring chain, leaves the Spindle
station at 13:00 (down, for Vexilla's standing grocery order) and 13:30 (up); anyone in it
rides. Or climb the chain (2,000 ft; DC 15 Athletics ×4; a fall is a fall). Or fly.

## Areas
- **the-landing** — the basket dock on the castle's underside; a hatch up into the cellars; grocery crates
- **the-great-hall** — stairs up from the cellar; the table for twelve; the rug; two flying swords over the fire
- **the-kitchen** — off the hall; the cold-pantry (a large chest, hungry)
- **the-workshop** — Vexilla's tower; benches, the Clapper under a label; Belvedere's station
- **the-garden** — the roof; frozen topiary; the chain's anchor-ring; a 2,000-ft view of the kingdom (the Observatory's dome is visible; so is the Notch)
