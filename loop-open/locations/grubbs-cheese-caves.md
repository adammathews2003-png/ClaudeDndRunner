# Mother Grubb's Cheese Caves

## Description
A limestone hollow a mile and a half east of town where Goat Hollow's goats are milked
and the milk goes into caves to become the kingdom's one export. Three caves run back
into the hill, cool and ammoniac, racked floor to ceiling with wheels in every stage of
rind. Mother Grubb lives in the front cave in a chair the size of a bed, and has one
problem this winter: rats, a great many, which came up from somewhere at the back of
the third cave and have begun eating the good stuff. Goats everywhere, including one
very old billy with a brass bell who stands in doorways and judges.

## Areas
- **the-front-cave** — Mother Grubb's chair, the press, the day's wheels; goats
- **the-second-cave** — the ageing racks; the Wheel of Heroism on the top shelf under a cloth
- **the-third-cave** — the back; the rats' hole; a draught from somewhere deeper (unexplored; not for this winter)

## Items & features
- The Wheel of Heroism (second cave): a cheese Mother Grubb made "the year the King was crowned"; eating a wedge = potion of heroism; four wedges in the wheel. Her reward for the rats, with potion of healing ×2 from her shelf and twenty wheels' worth of goodwill
- Mother Grubb's cheese: a wheel of it gets the party past Toby and makes Brother Anselm weep (glacier-shrine)
- Baaldur (front cave): the old billy; Phil's goat; Phil says Baaldur remembers. Baaldur chews
- The rats' hole (third cave): big enough to crawl; the draught smells of coins (it runs toward the Mint's lower galleries; not mapped, not for this winter)

## Hidden
- DC 11 (the-second-cave): the top-shelf cloth has been lifted recently; one wedge is already gone (Phil, several thousand loops ago; it grows back)
- DC 13 (the-third-cave): rat tracks come in on the draught, which means the hole goes somewhere
