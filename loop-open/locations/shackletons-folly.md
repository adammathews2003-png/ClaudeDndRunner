# Shackleton's Folly

## Description
A round stone tower, three storeys, built directly on the lake ice a mile and a half
out from the town stair, because Dame Ursula Shackleton, Royal Cartographer, wanted "a
clear horizon and no neighbours." It has both. Kennels on the ice beside it hold her
dog team, which is to say four wolves she has persuaded to pull a sledge. Inside: maps on
every wall, a stove, a chess table, a surveyor's theodolite pointed at the keep, and a
locked sea-trunk. The Folly groans when the ice does. Shackleton sets out for the south
pass every morning at ten with the dogs and comes back every afternoon at four, having
failed, which she regards as data.

## Areas
- **the-ice** — the kennels, the sledge, the door; the wolves are chained 06:00–10:00 and 16:00–24:00
- **the-map-room** — ground floor; the stove, the chess table, the trunk under the window
- **the-upper-rooms** — bed, instruments, the theodolite; a trapdoor to the roof

## Items & features
- **The Almanac of the Still Hour** (map-room, in the sea-trunk, DC 15 Thieves' Tools): Brahe's working notes, blue-bound; she took it on Day -12 when the Society expelled her. **Piece 3.** Reading it (1 hour, DC 12 Arcana or Investigation) gives: the Vigil's timetable (lens check 23:00, full spin 23:30, three turns of the Key to unwind), that a loud noise breaks the Fellows' chant and the Society must restart it (surprise round; the Fellows are out of it for two rounds), that a struck midnight breaks the fold, that the Star reflected back over-winds the Orrery, and a line in the margin in her hand: "AND STEP OUT OF THE RING, YOU IDIOT"
- The theodolite (upper rooms): aimed at the Observatory; her notes on the dome's lens slot (it opens at 23:00)
- The chess table (map-room): a game in progress against nobody; she will play anyone; she has never lost and says so

## Notes / current state
Shackleton gives the Almanac to anyone who (a) beats her at chess (she is a bandit
captain statblock with Int 14; a contest of Int, best of three), or (b) shows her a live
hazard stone and a plan she has not already tried (the party's loop knowledge counts), or
(c) Persuasion DC 13 after the party names Brahe as the problem. She wants to be told she
was right. She was.
