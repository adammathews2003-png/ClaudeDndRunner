# The Glacier Shrine

## Description
Four miles above Kettlebrook on Hermit's Shoulder, where the trail gives out and the
glacier begins, a shrine of the old Star-faith is cut into the rock: a round cell, a
stone altar, and above the altar a silvered disc that catches whatever is in the sky.
Brother Anselm has kept it for forty years, alone except for Toby, an ogre who turned up
one winter and stayed, took a vow of silence, and communicates with painted boards.
The glacier shelf above the shrine is home to ice mephits who regard the trail as
theirs. The wind here can lift a man. The view goes all the way down to the lake, and
at night the Observatory's dome is a bright brass pin far below.

## Areas
- **the-shelf** — the last 400 ft of trail across the glacier's lip; crevasses (DC 12 to notice; a fall is 30 ft); the mephits' ground
- **the-cell** — the shrine itself: altar, the disc, Anselm's bed, a fire, a great many cheese rinds
- **tobys-ledge** — a wide ledge beside the cell with a lean-to and a stack of painted boards (WELCOME, LEAVE, NO, SOUP?)

## Items & features
- **The Mirror of the Still Star** (cell): a silvered disc a foot across, mounted above the altar; "it returns the Star's gaze." Fits the great lens's cradle exactly. **Piece 4.** Anselm's to give; he gives it for a sincere promise that the Star will be left alone and a cheese (he has not had cheese since autumn). Persuasion DC 15 without the cheese. Theft brings Toby
- Anselm's star-tables (cell): the Star-faith's old astronomy, from which the Society "borrowed" everything; DC 12 Arcana to see the Working's timetable in them (a weaker substitute for the Almanac: timetable only, no chant-break, no ring warning)
- Toby's boards (ledge): WELCOME, LEAVE, NO, SOUP?, and one that says SORRY which he holds up after hitting someone

## Hidden

- DC 14 (the-cell): the disc's mounting has been unscrewed and screwed back recently, by someone with a Society tool (a brass star on the screw heads); Brahe tried to buy it on Day -30 and was refused
