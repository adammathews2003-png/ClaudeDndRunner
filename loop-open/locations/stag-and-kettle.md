# The Stag & Kettle

## Description
A fat, low inn on the east side of Kettle Square, three centuries old and leaning on the
building next door for support. The common room is all dark beams, a hearth you could
park a cart in, and a stag's head over the bar that somebody has dressed in a Midwinter
bonnet. Hob Gudgeon runs it; his daughter Marigold runs him. The cheap room is the loft:
four cots under the roof beams, reached by a ladder behind the bar, cold enough that the
party has slept in their boots. The yard behind holds Dolorous Pike's sledge, forty
wheels of cheese under oilcloth, and a mule with opinions.

## Areas
- **common-room** — front door from the square; the hub. Bar on the north wall, hearth east, notice board by the door
- **kitchen** — door in the common room's east wall; Hob's domain, Marigold's stage
- **the-loft** — ladder behind the bar, up through a hatch (z 15); four cots, one window onto the square. **The PCs' beds.** `loop-bed: stag-and-kettle/the-loft`
- **phils-cupboard** — the cupboard under the stairs, which Phil rents for a copper a day; sleeping-roll and a coat
- **stable-yard** — side door from the kitchen; gate on the south side onto Pike Lane; the sledge, the cheese, the mule
- **cellar** — trapdoor in the kitchen floor; ale, turnips, and the kettle grog barrel nobody admits to

- Notice board (by the door): Society notice "THE VIGIL OF THE STILL STAR — the Keep closed to the public from 20:00 by order of the Astronomer-Royal"; Pike's notice "GUARDS WANTED FOR THE SOUTH PASS, LEAVING DAWN, GOOD MONEY LATER"
- Bar: the stag's head; a strongbox (DC 15 Thieves' Tools), ~30 gp and Hob's recipe for kettle grog (illegible)
- The loft: four cots, four blankets; on loop 0 a rope round every PC's ankles, the far end tied to Phil
- Stable yard: Pike's sledge (bell on the harness; rings at 06:30 when he leaves), 40 wheels of Grubb cheese
- Phil's cupboard: a coat with hundreds of folded papers sewn into the lining (persistent; each a plan, each crossed out); a Marchwarden's hazard stone in the pocket until loop 1 (then the party's)

## Hidden

- DC 14 (common-room): the stag's bonnet is pinned with a brass Society lapel-pin (a star in a circle), left by a Fellow
- DC 13 (phils-cupboard): the coat's lining crackles; the papers inside are all in the same hand
