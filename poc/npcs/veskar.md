# Veskar

## Description
Lean, soft-spoken man of indeterminate middle age; travel-worn clothes that were once
expensive. Smiles readily; the smile never reaches his eyes. Pays with old-minted silver.

## Personality & motivation
Patient, professional, unsentimental. In Thornbury on business he intends to finish
quietly (see scenario — The truth). Violence is a cost, not a pleasure; he will bribe,
misdirect, and stall before he draws steel. If cornered, he negotiates first, fights
without mercy second.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares (all lies, DC 14 Insight): he's a grain buyer from the city; Harl sold him stock and mentioned traveling to see a sister
- Under pressure: nothing. He deflects, then removes himself. Direct evidence makes him bolt for the mill.

## Movements
Keeps to room 3 by day. Out from midnight to pre-dawn (the mill, via the stable yard).
If suspicion rises (GM judgment / scenario beat 3), he attempts to leave Thornbury by night.
