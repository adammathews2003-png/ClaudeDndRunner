# Tobin Hale

## Description
19, gangly, straw-colored hair, forever smelling of horses. Talks too much, especially
after two ales, which is most evenings.

## Personality & motivation
Desperate to matter. Harl the miller was kind to him, and Tobin genuinely wants him
found — he just isn't brave enough to go poking around alone. Will latch onto anyone
who treats him as competent.

## Knowledge & secrets
- Freely shares (unprompted, if anyone listens): he saw a cart leave the square toward the mill road two nights ago, well after midnight — no lantern
- Needs friendly chat (no roll if kind to him): the cart belonged to no one in the village; the driver wore a hood, and a second shape sat slumped beside him
- Doesn't realize he knows (DC 10 Insight while he rambles): the "slumped shape" wore Harl's red scarf
