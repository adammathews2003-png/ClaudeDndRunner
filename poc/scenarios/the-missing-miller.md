# The Missing Miller

## Premise (player-safe)
Harl the miller hasn't been seen in a week. His mill wheel still turns, his pipe lies on
the floor, and the village reeve has posted 25 gp for word of him. The village is uneasy;
nobody wants to walk the mill road after dark.

## The truth (SPOILERS — GM only)
Veskar is an agent of the Red Ledger, a smuggling ring using rural mills as waypoints.
Harl discovered contraband hidden in his under-croft and Veskar took him. Harl is alive,
manacled beneath the Crossroads Inn's cellar (Veskar copied Mara's key), moved there
because the mill felt exposed. Veskar is waiting for a Red Ledger cart in three days to
remove Harl — and the contraband — permanently.

## Factions & relationships
- **Veskar / Red Ledger** — wants: clean extraction in 3 days; fears: the reeve raising a militia; will: bribe, frame someone, or kill Harl if exposure is imminent
- **Mara** — knows something is wrong under her inn, too scared to speak; protects the village first
- **Tobin** — saw the night cart; wants to be a hero, needs backup
- **Reeve Odell** (not yet detailed — stub if used) — posted the reward; cautious, by-the-book

## Beats & triggers  <!-- mirror active ones into state/current.md "Watch for" -->
- WHEN party asks Mara about Harl → she deflects (Deception; DC 12 Insight catches the lie) → opens the "something's wrong at the inn" thread
- WHEN party is kind to Tobin or buys him a drink → cart story, unprompted
- WHEN party enters the mill → physical evidence chain (pipe, blood DC 11, ledger "V.", under-croft manacle)
- WHEN party opens the inn cellar → Harl found; Veskar knows within the hour and bolts or bargains
- WHEN suspicion of Veskar becomes open (accusation, searched room) → he attempts night departure; confrontation on the mill road
- CLOCK Day 3 04:00: Red Ledger cart arrives at the mill; if Harl is still captive, he is gone for good (scenario fails forward into a rescue-from-the-road arc)

## Resolution paths
1. **Harl rescued, Veskar caught/killed** → village grateful; Red Ledger notes the party's names (future hook)
2. **Harl rescued, Veskar escapes** → lingering threat; Mara becomes a staunch ally
3. **Clock expires** → chase/rescue arc on the road; Thornbury sours on outsiders
