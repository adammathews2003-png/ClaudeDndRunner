## Background & story
Grew up running errands, and worse, for smugglers in a river city's dock district, then
walked away when a job got someone killed. Now she takes honest(ish) work: finding
things, and people, that are lost. She came to Thornbury on the reeve's notice.
- **Hook:** the crews she ran with paid in old-minted silver, coin that couldn't be
  traced to anyone respectable. She'll recognize it on sight.
- **Goal:** prove to herself she's on the right side now; a rescue would count.
