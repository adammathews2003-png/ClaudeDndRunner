## Features & abilities
- **Disciple of Life:** healing spells of 1st level or higher restore an extra
  2 + the spell's level HP.
- **Channel Divinity (1/short rest):**
  - *Preserve Life:* restore up to 15 HP split among creatures within 30 ft, none above half max.
  - *Turn Undead:* undead within 30 ft, WIS save DC 13 or turned for 1 min.
- **Heavy armor proficiency** (Life Domain).

## Background & story
A young priest of Chauntea, the grain goddess, sent from the temple in the market town
to see to Thornbury's little shrine before harvest. Earnest, steady, a better listener
than a talker.
- **Hook:** every harvest, Harl the miller milled the shrine's offering-grain for free.
  The temple's letters to him went unanswered this month. Kael is here partly to find
  out why.
- **Goal:** leave the village better tended than he found it, crops and people both.
