# Thornbury Village Square

## Description
A packed-dirt square ringed by a dozen buildings: the Crossroads Inn to the east, a
smithy, a small shrine to Chauntea, and the reeve's house with its slate roof. A dry
stone well sits at the center. Population ~200; everyone knows everyone, and strangers
are watched with polite suspicion.

## Areas
- **square** — the open square itself; the well at the center. Ways out (the inn, the mill road, the reeve's house) are Thornbury's Routes

## Items & features
- Well (center): rope and bucket; nothing unusual
- Shrine of Chauntea: candle-lit, tended daily

## Hidden
- DC 12: cart tracks come in off the mill road and cross the square toward the back of the inn — made at night (frost pattern), on the night of Day -1
