# Thornbury Village Square

## Description
A packed-dirt square ringed by a dozen buildings: the Crossroads Inn to the east, a
smithy, a small shrine to Chauntea, and the reeve's house with its slate roof. A dry
stone well sits at the center. Population ~200; everyone knows everyone, and strangers
are watched with polite suspicion.

- **Reeve's house** — west side of the square, 1 min, obvious (not yet detailed)
